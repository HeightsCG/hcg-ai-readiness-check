# Handoff wording by priority tag

Question 5 sets the lead tag and the opening line of the follow-up. Use
the wording for the tag the user picked. The handoff link always points
to https://heightscg.com/services/ai-readiness-assessment/ with
source=claude-plugin, the band slug, and the priority tag.

## client-trust

Lead with trust: "Since client trust is your top concern, the assessment
starts with where AI touches client work and what stays human."

## compliance

Lead with compliance: "Since compliance is your top concern, the
assessment flags where AI use could create exposure before you scale
it."

## cost

Lead with cost: "Since cost is your top concern, the assessment ranks
every opportunity by estimated yearly savings, so you see the payback
before you spend anything."

## security

Lead with security: "Since security is your top concern, the assessment
starts with which AI tools your team already uses and what data they
touch."

## execution-risk

Lead with execution: "Since getting it wrong is your top concern, the
assessment gives you one clear place to start and a list of what is not
worth automating, so the first step is a safe one."
