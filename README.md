# AI Readiness Check by Heights Consulting Group

Most owner-led firms are already using AI, with or without a plan. The AI
Readiness Check runs a short, structured check across strategy, data,
governance, people, and risk, then gives you a banded result and the
priority moves that matter for a business your size.

It takes under three minutes, needs no sensitive data, and collects
nothing. Your answers stay in your chat unless you choose to book the
$99 AI Readiness Assessment at
https://heightscg.com/services/ai-readiness-assessment/, where Heights
Consulting Group reviews how your business runs and returns a written
report with ranked savings, the tools that do the work, what they cost,
and the math behind every number.

## How to use it

Install the plugin, then ask Claude to check if your business is ready
for AI, run the AI readiness check, or score your AI governance. Answer
five questions, one at a time, and you get your result in one message.

## What data it sends

Nothing. Version 1 has no server, no login, and no data collection. The
only link it offers is to the assessment page on heightscg.com, and only
if you choose to click it.
